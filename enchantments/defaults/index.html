    Default enchantments¶

    These are the 112 enchantments that come with ComplexEnchants (they are installed on a new server, in plugins/ComplexEnchants/enchants/, and you can change or delete any of them). Values that change with the level are shown for every level ("10/20/30%") or from the first to the last level ("1–5").
